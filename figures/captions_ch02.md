## Figure 2.2 — Fig02-02_qpcr-indicators
Anchor: Section 2.3, after "…neither measure alone is a definitive diagnosis [Hud08, Ewi16]."
Caption: **Figure 2.2. Reading qPCR quality indicators.** Schematic amplification curves for a short human target, a long human target and an internal PCR control (IPC) in an intact, a degraded and an inhibited extract. Delayed amplification of the long target relative to the short target suggests degradation, whereas a delayed IPC suggests inhibition; neither pattern alone is a definitive diagnosis. Simulated data for illustration. Source: Original illustration based on [Hud08, Ewi16].
Interactive candidate: yes (degradation/inhibition sliders).

## Figure 2.3 — Fig02-03_str-multiplex
Anchor: Section 2.4, after "…or alter amplification and allele designation [But06c, But12e]."
Caption: **Figure 2.3. STR markers and multiplex design.** (A) Alleles differ in the number of repeat units, so length-based typing separates them; a same-length allele with an internal sequence variant is not distinguished by length. (B) In a multiplex, loci are distinguished by fragment-size range and fluorescent dye channel, with an internal size standard as a within-run size reference; amelogenin X and Y products differ in length. Repeat motif, sizes and layout are illustrative and do not represent a specific commercial kit. Source: Original illustration based on [But06c, But12e, But12f, But12b, But14].
Interactive candidate: no.

## Figure 2.4 — Fig02-04_ce-sizing
Anchor: Section 2.5, after "…can make apparent size alone an imperfect basis for naming an allele [But12b]."
Caption: **Figure 2.4. Capillary electrophoresis and allele designation.** (A) Fluorescently labelled amplicons (dye colours illustrative) migrate through a polymer-filled capillary, shorter fragments generally faster, and are detected by laser-induced fluorescence at a detection window. (B) The internal size standard converts migration into estimated fragment size (1); allele designation then compares the sized fragment with the allelic ladder (2). Simulated data for illustration. Source: Original illustration based on [But12b, Mor01].
Interactive candidate: yes (migration animation).
